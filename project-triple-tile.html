Triple Tile 3D: Match Puzzle

ESTIMATED DEVELOPMENT TIME2 weeks
PLATFORMAndroid
APPROACHBuilt from scratch

A three-of-a-kind puzzle with a limited collection tray. Players choose tiles from the board and clear matching triples before the tray fills with unmatched pieces. Hints, shuffle and undo provide ways to recover while preserving the need to plan each selection.
