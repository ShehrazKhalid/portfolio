Card Sort Puzzle Shuffle Sort

ESTIMATED DEVELOPMENT TIME2 weeks
PLATFORMAndroid
APPROACHBuilt from scratch

A card-sorting puzzle that combines colour, number and merge rules. Move matching cards between decks, combine them for rewards and unlock further cards as the collection grows. Simple actions create a longer planning challenge as more colours and numbers enter play.
