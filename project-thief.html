Robbery Simulator: Thief House

Also referred to as Thief Simulator

ESTIMATED DEVELOPMENT TIME4 weeks
PLATFORMAndroid
APPROACHBuilt from scratch

A first-person theft simulation built around exploring locations, collecting valuables and completing heists. My from-scratch build includes an inventory, pickable items and a day/night cycle. These systems give the player a connected sequence of preparation, interaction and escape within the game world.
