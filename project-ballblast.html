Cannon Blast Master

Also referred to as Ball Blast / Royal Smash

ESTIMATED DEVELOPMENT TIME1 week
PLATFORMAndroid
APPROACHBuilt from scratch

A physics-based cannon game about lining up a shot and bringing structures down. Aim, fire and knock targets apart across short stages, with obstacles that reward timing and accuracy. I built the game from scratch, including the 3D assets, with a focus on satisfying impacts and clear level objectives.
