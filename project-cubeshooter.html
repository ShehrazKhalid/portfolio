BloxStrike

ESTIMATED DEVELOPMENT TIME2 weeks
PLATFORMAndroid
APPROACHBuilt from scratch

A compact, block-styled first-person shooter built around team combat, readable arenas and weapon handling. My original implementation uses offline 5v5 matches: the player and four AI teammates face five opponents. Custom navigation, target selection and shooting connect the combat systems into a complete match.
