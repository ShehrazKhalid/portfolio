About this game

BloxStrike is a fast-paced, competitive 5v5 shooter where strategy, aim, and teamwork decide the outcome.

Battle across detailed maps in intense round-based combat. Eliminate opponents, plant or defuse the objective, and outplay the enemy team.

Earn cash each round by performing well—then spend them on a wide arsenal of weapons, utility, and gear to gain the upper hand in future rounds. Featuring an evolving weapon skin economy, unique gameplay, and regular updates, BloxStrike delivers a next-generation FPS experience.

Key Game Features
  • Fast-paced 5v5 action
  • Advanced AI bots mimicking real players
  • Engaging multiplayer feel without relying on servers
  • Developed from scratch with highly modular code

Developer Insights & Post-Mortem

The 8-Day Crunch Challenge

This entire project was conceptualized, developed, and published in a rapid, highly intensive cycle of just 8 days. Building a fully functional 3D shooter in such a tight timeframe required intense focus, quick decision-making, and writing highly modular and reusable code from scratch.

Custom Built AI Systems

Instead of relying on pre-built assets, all AI behaviors were programmed entirely from scratch. The AI agents are capable of dynamic pathfinding, taking cover, acquiring targets, and shooting strategically. They behave so organically that players often forget they aren't playing against real human opponents.

The 5v5 Multiplayer Illusion

A core goal was to make this offline game feel like a lively multiplayer match. The game features a 5v5 team deathmatch format where the player fights alongside 4 AI companions against 5 AI enemies. The constant interaction between the agents ensures the battlefield always feels alive and reactive.

Trade-offs & Achievements

Due to the strict 8-day deadline, some secondary features had to be skipped, such as an introductory tutorial sequence. However, this allowed me to pour all my effort into the core gameplay loop. By maximizing interactivity and responsive shooting mechanics, the game remains highly engaging despite the rapid development cycle.
