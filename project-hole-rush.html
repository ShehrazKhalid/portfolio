Hole Rush: Jungle Master

ESTIMATED DEVELOPMENT TIME2 weeks
PLATFORMAndroid
APPROACHBuilt from scratch

Control a growing hole through jungle levels and consume objects in the environment. Start with small items, increase in size and work towards larger obstacles. Planning a route through objects of the right size creates a clear collection-and-growth loop.
